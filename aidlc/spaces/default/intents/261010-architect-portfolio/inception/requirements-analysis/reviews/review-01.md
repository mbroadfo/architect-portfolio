| R-01 | Major | aidlc/spaces/default/intents/261010-architect-portfolio/inception/requirements-analysis/requirements.md > FR1.1 | "The single configured owner identity" does not say how the owner is identified. This is the main gate for the whole Studio. | State that the owner is matched on the identity provider's immutable subject ID (or a verified email), that self-signup is disabled on the tenant, and add a Given/When/Then for an unverified or look-alike identity being refused. | New |
| R-02 | Major | aidlc/spaces/default/intents/261010-architect-portfolio/inception/requirements-analysis/requirements.md > FR4.2, FR4.4 | Owner-uploaded workshop and CAD photos become public as-is. ### Summary

The requirements are specific, traceable to the charter, the D-01 to D-22 decisions and the Q&A answers, and they respect the owner's decisions on cost, SSM credentials, static export and uploaded images. Two security and privacy gaps are worth the owner's attention before approval: how the owner identity is bound, and the exposure of image metadata at publish. The rest are minor tightenings that can be absorbed in design.
