### Findings

| ID | Severity | Location | Finding | Required action |

| A1-S01 | Major | NFR17, bullet 2 | The address rule is a deny-list of named classes. Forms that commonly bypass such lists are not named, and none of them is testable as written: IPv4-mapped and IPv4-embedding IPv6 (`::ffff:0:0/96`, `64:ff9b::/96`, `2002::/16`, Teredo `2001::/32`); `0.0.0.0/8`, `198.18.0.0/15`, `192.0.0.0/24`, `240.0.0.0/4` and broadcast; ULA `fc00::/7`, which includes the AWS IPv6 metadata address `fd00:ec2::254` ("private" is ambiguous for IPv6). The text also does not say what happens when a hostname resolves to several addresses, or how URLs are parsed: userinfo, decimal, octal or hex IPv4 literals, IDN and trailing dots. | Reword it as an allow-list: "Connect only to addresses that are globally routable unicast under the IANA IPv4 and IPv6 special-purpose registries. Normalize IPv4-mapped and embedded IPv6 to the IPv4 address before checking. Refuse the URL if any resolved address is refused. Parse the URL with one canonical parser, and reject URLs that contain userinfo or a non-canonical IP literal." |

The request asks for `Accept-Encoding: identity`, and the 256 KB cap applies to the bytes after any decoding." |

### Summary

A1 keeps the approved boundaries in intent, and several of the new controls are strong: the attachment-only downloads, the "Reachable" labelling and the review requirement for the validator. As written, though, the amendment leaves gaps that could expose private material or allow server-side request forgery (SSRF):

- **NFR17 (link check):** bypass forms are not named (A1-S01); the TLS, timeout and decompression rules are missing (A1-S02); page rendering is not ruled out (A1-S03); and nothing limits what a bypass can reach (A1-S04).
- **CR-02 (new upload formats):** the parsers have no limits (A1-S07, A1-S08), and attachment metadata is published unseen (A1-S09).
- **CR-03 and CR-01 (metadata and Risk):** the public export has no field allow-list (A1-S11); a failed visibility check does not fail safe (A1-S12); and Risk's withholding covers the site only, not the public repository (A1-S13).
- **CR-05 (featured and categories):** they have no explicit publish path (A1-S14), and take-down leaves stale references in the carousel and on category pages (A1-S15).
- **CR-04 (visual identity):** the referenced prototype brings in third-party font requests (A1-S16).

The Minor findings (A1-S05, A1-S06, A1-S10) are precision fixes. Every finding can be fixed with the concise wording proposed, and none reopens an owner decision. Re-review the changed text once it is applied.
