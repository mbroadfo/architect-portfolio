### Notes

Three things hold up. R-07 is resolved: the IAM scope is two prefixes, the key checks are specified, and tests are listed. R-08 is resolved: all routes go through U3's pipeline with no opt-out. The snapshot enforces the FR4.10 allow-list structurally. Consistency with ADR-002, ADR-004 and ADR-012 is good: the publish request and result support per-attempt tokens and fencing, and confirmations are idempotent.

The three Major findings are gaps at unit boundaries, not mechanism choices that can wait. Closing them lets U5, U7, U8 and U9 be built in parallel from U1. Environment wiring between U2 and the other units (bucket names, invoker policy, parameter names) is left to Infrastructure Design, which is reasonable.
