CS-13 (sixth featured) and CS-14 (Update after an accepted take-down) cover the two original failures, and CS-11 now agrees with the run-time check.

> R-09 Reviewer note: The guard's conditional write does not bump the revision, every composition and flag edit does, and CS-15 matches ADR-012.

> R-10 Reviewer note: Release of the draft featured slot is now in the commit transaction (CS-16). Take-down retries are inside the budget, and the exhausted case releases the lease so the queue moves on.
