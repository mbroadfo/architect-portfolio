| R-01 | Major | aidlc/spaces/default/intents/261010-architect-portfolio/inception/domain-design/components.md > component Publication behaviour ("On confirmation it freezes a PublishedVersion"); decisions.md > ADR-002 | The design never says the owner's confirmation is tied to the draft revision whose inventory was shown. Preparation builds the inventory (including the live-URL check, repository visibility and attachment entries) at one moment. Confirmation then freezes a PublishedVersion. ### Summary

The boundaries are sound and match the requirements. Owner access, uploads, SSRF isolation and the publication boundary each have a single owner, and the ADRs follow the required structure with honest alternatives. Two gaps weigh on the publication boundary and the owner should weigh them before approving: confirmation is not bound to the reviewed draft revision, and shared public pages are not serialized across exhibits. Both can be fixed with a sentence each, so the verdict is READY.
