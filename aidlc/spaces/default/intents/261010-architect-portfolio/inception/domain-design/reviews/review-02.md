> R-01 Reviewer note: Preparation freezes the candidate version and the inventory digest in a single-use ticket. Acceptance checks the digest and the Projects revision guard together, and ADR-012 and scenarios CS-01 to CS-04 state this. Two small gaps remain as new findings: what the guard's conditional write does to the revision, and what the Publish and Update tickets do not bind to.

> R-02 Reviewer note: ADR-004 now has one site-wide sequence, a fresh start from the last committed state, and a fenced export. The fencing mechanism has its own flaws, listed under New findings.

> R-03 Reviewer note: Publication is named the only builder of the public site model. Categories are frozen into each version. Take-down and publish both update PublishedFeaturedOrder, and a newly featured exhibit is appended.

> R-04 Reviewer note: Repositories reach a version only through publicRepositories. A free-form link to a repository host blocks Publish, and PB-03 covers it.

> R-05 Reviewer note: Take-down failure behaviour and status are stated in the Publication block and CS-10. The health endpoint is exempted explicitly and returns no data (PB-11).

> R-06 Reviewer note: Projects refuses an asset from another project (PB-10). The Publication to PublicSiteExporter call is marked async.
