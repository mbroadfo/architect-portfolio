# Intent Statement — Architect's Portfolio

## Problem Statement

Michael Broadfoot needs a personal architect's portfolio: a public, image-rich, exhibition-style site at michael.broadfoot.consulting, paired with a private administration Studio for curating what it shows. [desc]

The portfolio addresses four problems the owner confirmed: [Q1]

- Michael's work is spread across GitHub, Google Drive, SketchUp and the workshop, and no single place presents it as a coherent body of work. [Q1]
- A résumé or LinkedIn profile cannot convey how Michael thinks and builds, so a credible showcase is needed that supports consulting or advisory work. [Q1]
- Michael wants a place to share selected projects, including build-it-yourself designs, with peers and the maker community. [Q1]
- Keeping project write-ups current is tedious, so curating and publishing must take little effort, with AI assistance where it helps. [Q1] [desc]

## Target Customer

The public portfolio serves these visitors:

| Audience | What they come for | When served | Source |
|----------|--------------------|-------------|--------|
| Prospective consulting clients, hiring managers, and organizations evaluating advisory work | A credible picture of what Michael designs and builds, and how he thinks | First version | [Q2] [Q1] |
| Fellow engineers and architects | The technical and architectural thinking behind each project | First version | [Q9] |
| Makers, woodworkers and CNC/CAD hobbyists | Enough build-oriented detail to build or adapt the designs | First version | [Q9] [Q1] |
| Family, friends and specific invited people | Content shared with them by invitation | A later version, not the first | [Q2] [Q10] |

Because makers and engineers are part of the audience alongside clients and employers, exhibits need to support both outcome-level reading and deeper technical and build detail. [Q9]

Michael is also a beneficiary as the site's curator: the private Studio exists to make discovering, describing and publishing Michael's projects low-effort. [Q1] [desc]

## Success Metrics

Success is judged only by signals that need no behavioral tracking, visitor profiling or per-visitor data. [Q3]

| Metric | How it is measured | Target | Source |
|--------|--------------------|--------|--------|
| Seed exhibits published | Count of the three seed exhibits (AI Arcade, STARS, Mom's Recipe Box) live on the public site | All three published | [Q3] |
| Effort to publish a new exhibit | Owner's own time from a discovered GitHub repository to a published exhibit, once the Studio exists | To be set after the first slice is in use | [Q3] [Q11] |
| Exhibit reach | Total requests per exhibit, aggregated from content-delivery logs, with no cookies, no per-visitor identifiers and no IP addresses retained | No target set; tracked as a trend | [Q3] |

## Initiative Trigger

Four drivers make this the right time: [Q4]

- Establishing a consulting presence under the broadfoot.consulting domain. [Q4]
- The body of work (AI Arcade, STARS, Mom's Recipe Box and others) deserves a proper home. [Q4]
- The portfolio itself is a showcase of AI-assisted, agent-driven development using AI-DLC. [Q4]
- A personal milestone or career transition. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

**Workflow-selected scope:** `classic`, run as a tailored plan that covers framing, full Inception, the first vertical slice, and gated deployment. [scope]

**User-confirmed product boundary for the first version:**

- In: the first vertical slice described in charter section 8, delivered after Inception. [desc]
