# Rough Mockups — Questions

The owner asked (2026-10-10) that routine decisions be made autonomously from the approved charter, recorded decisions and sensible defaults, with only genuinely unresolved questions put to him. Every question below is answered from those sources; each answer names its source. The one open design choice (which gallery concept to carry forward) is presented at the approval gate instead of here.

## Q1. What are the primary entry points and key screens?

A. Public: gallery home and exhibit page. Studio: sign-in, project list, repository discovery, exhibit editor (with AI suggestion and preview), publish confirmation
B. Public gallery only; the Studio is designed later
C. Studio only; the public side is designed later
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — from the scope document (In Scope: Private Studio and Public site) and intent backlog IB-1 to IB-15.

## Q2. What is the core user flow (happy path)?

A. Owner: sign in, discover repository, create or link project, get AI suggestion, edit, preview, publish; then a visitor opens the gallery, picks a card, reads the exhibit

C. Owner flow only
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — from the scope document's Value Stream and charter §8.

## Q3. What does the information hierarchy look like?

A. Public: gallery of image-led cards, then exhibit page (lead image and summary first, narrative sections, gallery, links, build-it-yourself material). Studio: projects, then one project's exhibit editor
B. Text-first résumé layout
C. Category taxonomy first (disciplines), then projects
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — charter §1 ("visitors encounter the creations immediately"), §3 (narrative sections), scope document (image-card gallery; richer grouping and filtering deferred).

## Q4. Are there brand guidelines, design systems or UI patterns to follow?

B. A third-party design system
C. None
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — charter §1 and §3.

## Q5. What devices and form factors must be supported?

A. Responsive: phone, tablet and desktop for the public site; desktop-first for the Studio, usable on tablet
B. Desktop only
C. Mobile only
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — sensible default: public visitors arrive on any device; the Studio is a curation workspace used by the owner at a desk. Requirements Analysis can tighten this.

## Q6. Are there known accessibility requirements?

A. WCAG 2.1 AA for the public site and the Studio: keyboard-operable, visible focus, alt text on every exhibit image, contrast at least 4.5:1 on the dark theme, reduced-motion respected
B. No specific level
C. WCAG 2.1 AAA
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — sensible default and the design practice baseline; confirmed or tightened in Requirements Analysis.
