The entity count (45) matches the summary.

What held up: R-07 is fully covered. Layer 1 (BR3.5) and layer 2 checks (a) to (d) map to BR3.3, BR3.4 and BR3.6, and every K case maps to a rule. The R-08 registry equality, four non-owner calls, spy handlers and single-server rules are present. R-18 points 1 and 2 are named (three channels, shared Error body, 303 exception). The snapshot allow-list, closed-schema, no-address and preview-versus-publish separation rules (BR1.3, BR1.4, BR2.4, BR5.6) are sound, U8 sees only the approved snapshot, and the port providers and consumers match the approved Call Direction table for the ports it lists. The BR7.6 clarification (ASCII-only fold, hyphen trim after the cut) is a reasonable and disclosed tightening.

Verdict basis: there are five Major findings (more than two), so the verdict is NOT-READY. The first, fourth and fifth are internal contradictions or unsatisfiable tests in rules that carry R-18 and the strictness model. The second leaves the ADR-004 stalled-attempt guarantee untested. The third leaves B02's own acceptance criteria unprovable at the U1 checkpoint. Each is fixable by editing the artifacts, with no change to an approved contract.
