### Notes

All twelve prior findings are Fixed against the revised text; the verdict basis is therefore one open Major and the Minor items above (READY allows up to two Major). I read rules.md, entities.md, functional-spec.md and traceability.json against contract-summary C1 to C9, ADR-003 and ADR-004, and the unit-of-work Call Direction table.

Disclosed tightenings. (1) Equal token: ADR-004 and C3 say tokens are strictly increasing per attempt and the fence refuses lower tokens. Refusing an equal token from another operationId follows from that. Accepting an equal token for the same operationId is an allowance for a duplicate delivery, not a tightening, and the label in BR3.1 is slightly wrong; it does not overrule ADR-004. (2) Recording the fence only after full validation, and the extra cases C3-F2 to C3-F5, add to the approved list without contradicting it. (3) Adding 500 INTERNAL as a shared response matches the approved status table. (4) The ASCII fold in BR7.6 is a narrowing reading of "lowercases" and is disclosed in both BR7.6 and the replacement table; I treat it as acceptable once the contract summary carries the clarification. None of these overrules an approved decision.

Traceability. All 41 acceptance criteria appear in traceability.json with status OK, and each target list equals the Rules column of the same criterion in functional-spec.md (checked by script, no mismatch). Every rule BR1.1 to BR9.6 (66) is cited by at least one acceptance criterion, no criterion cites an unknown rule, and the FR entries are marked N/A with the owning unit.

The one Major (the BR4.9 name test) is the same class of defect as iteration 1 R-04 and should be fixed before the gate; the other items are clarifications that can be taken in the same edit.
