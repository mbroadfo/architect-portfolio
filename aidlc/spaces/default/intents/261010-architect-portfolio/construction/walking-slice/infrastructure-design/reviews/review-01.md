### Notes

The design correctly implies nothing is deployed or paid: cost is zero, there are no secrets or cloud credentials, and cloud resources are deferred to U2. It is consistent with the NFR design on loopback binding, the .invalid alias, the relative symbolic link on a named volume, and the least-privilege and no pull_request_target rules. The gate rule (fail on cancelled, failed, or skipped-when-required) is sound. The verdict is NOT-READY because three Major findings exceed the limit of two: the unreachable fake issuer from the host browser, the replica-set start-up ordering and idempotency, and the review check's base.
